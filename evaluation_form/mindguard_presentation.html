Introduction

Background to the Field

🌍

Global Mental Health Crisis

Depression is the leading cause of disability worldwide, affecting 280 million people. Adolescents are disproportionately impacted, with 1 in 7 aged 10–19 experiencing a mental disorder.

📱

Adolescents & Online Communication

Adolescents spend an average of 7+ hours/day online. They frequently express emotional distress through written online text — often their primary outlet for self-expression.

⚠️

The Intervention Gap

Existing mental health tools rely on active user engagement, which fails for at-risk individuals who disengage when most distressed. Early detection requires passive, non-intrusive monitoring.

"The digital footprint left by adolescents during online communication represents an untapped early-warning signal for identifying depressive risk before it escalates to crisis."

03
Statement of Problem

The Problem We Solve

1
Existing mental health digital tools rely on active user engagement — apps, journaling, and chatbots only work if the user chooses to engage. Adolescents experiencing depressive episodes are the least likely to do so.
(Q. Guo et al., 2024; Hollis et al., 2021)
2
Adolescents express distress passively and incidentally through everyday online writing — social media posts, search queries, and typed messages — yet no browser-based tool exists to capture and analyse this signal.
(O'Reilly et al., 2023; Torous et al., 2021)
3
State-of-the-art detection models lack an integrated guardian/parent escalation framework — detection alone does not bridge the critical gap between identifying risk and real-world intervention.
(Zhang et al., 2023)
4
Current tools are not specifically designed for adolescents and do not account for the informal, colloquial, and coded language patterns unique to teenage online communication.
(Thabrew et al., 2022)
04
Aim

What We Set Out to Achieve

🎯

To design, develop, and evaluate a browser extension that passively monitors online text for depressive language patterns in adolescents, using a RAG-driven Large Language Model, and automatically notifies designated guardians when high-risk indicators are detected.

🔍 Passive Monitoring 🤖 RAG + LLM 🛡️ Risk Classification 🔔 Guardian Alerts
05
Objectives

Steps Taken to Achieve the Aim

I
Collect and pre-process a suitable dataset of depressive and non-depressive text samples representative of adolescent online communication (Dreaddit dataset; 2,838 samples).
II
Design and develop a trained risk classification model and evaluate the accuracy and reliability of the model's risk predictions using a hybrid NLP + RAG pipeline.
III
Implement the model within a browser extension architecture, incorporating a secure guardian notification framework and consent management system.
IV
Evaluate the usability of the system interface using the System Usability Scale (SUS) with 24 participants from the target demographic.
06
Methodology

How Each Objective Was Achieved

Obj I — Dataset
Dreaddit Reddit dataset · 2,838 samples · Colloquial adolescent-style text
Method
Text cleaning, stopword removal, lemmatisation. FAISS embedding index built from processed text using sentence-transformer embeddings.
Obj II — Model
3-class Risk Engine: Low / Moderate / High. Hybrid NLP scoring + RAG retrieval.
Method
RAG Pipeline: FAISS vector search retrieves semantically similar clinical cases → LLM (Gemini 1.5 Flash) reasons over context → produces risk level + explanation. 14-day temporal window (aligned to DSM-5 criteria).
Obj III — System
Browser extension (JavaScript), FastAPI backend, Guardian dashboard (HTML/JS).
Method
Design Science Research (DSR) methodology. Passive text interception → encrypted API call → risk classification → conditional guardian email alert with consent verification.
Obj IV — Evaluation
SUS questionnaire + model benchmarking on 42-sample held-out test set.
Method
Quantitative: Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-Score, Confusion Matrix. Qualitative: 10-item SUS with 24 participants on live extension + guardian dashboard.

Significance

Contribution & Impact

For the User
👧

Zero-Effort Protection

Adolescents receive protection without any active engagement or self-reporting — removing the biggest barrier to mental health support.

👨‍👩‍👧

Empowered Guardians

Parents and carers receive timely, actionable alerts, enabling earlier conversations and professional referrals before crises escalate.

🔒

Privacy-First Design

Explicit consent architecture ensures users retain full control over their data, building trust that existing monitoring tools lack.

For the Field
🔬

Novel RAG Application

First application of FAISS-backed RAG retrieval to passive browser telemetry for adolescent mental health — a new paradigm in Digital Phenotyping.

🏥

Clinical Safety Framework

Explicit ≥90% High-Risk Recall target aligned with clinical triage standards — bridging the gap between AI detection and clinical safety benchmarks.

🔭

Explainability Roadmap

User-driven recommendation for an XAI Insights Panel transforms the dashboard from an alerting tool to an educational clinical instrument.

10
Conclusion

What Was Accomplished

✔ Developed a fully functional browser extension that passively detects depressive language in adolescents' online text with 88.1% overall accuracy.
✔ Implemented a novel RAG-driven LLM pipeline using FAISS and Gemini, achieving a clinically critical High-Risk Recall of 94.1% (surpassing the ≥90% safety target).
✔ Built a complete guardian escalation framework with a real-time dashboard, consent management, and automated risk alert notifications.
✔ Validated usability with 24 participants achieving a SUS score of 69.1 (Above Average) — confirming the system is accessible to non-technical users.
✔ Identified key future work: an Explainable AI (XAI) insights panel to display linguistic triggers, improving transparency based on direct user feedback.
🎯 Overall: MindGuard successfully addresses the critical gap in passive, non-intrusive mental health monitoring for adolescents, demonstrating that a browser-based, RAG-augmented LLM system can detect depressive risk reliably and escalate it effectively to guardians.
